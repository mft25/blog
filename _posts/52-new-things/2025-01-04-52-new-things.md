**Happy New Year!**

As we step into 2025, I'm keeping a resolution from previous years: to go outside every day.
There are usually only a handful of days I miss, mostly when I'm unwell, so while I could tick it off
it's a habit I value and want to keep going.

This year, Sophie and I are adding a new resolution: to do *one new thing* each week.
It doesn't have to be anything big — maybe trying a new restaurant or visiting one of Oxford's many museums.
The idea is simply to encourage ourselves to seek out fresh experiences more regularly.

I've already started jotting down and categorising ideas.
Most of them are things I've been meaning to try for a while, 
so this is more about giving myself a gentle push than stepping out of my comfort zone.

To get started, I've decided to write this blog post — my first ever!
Setting up the blog on my domain was simple after finding this 
[helpful article](https://shahrajat.com/2016-06-22-install-jekyll-subdirectory-blog-github-pages/), 
though there's still some work to do to make it fit in with the rest of my website.

Here's to trying new things and seeing where the year takes us.
