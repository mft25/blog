Happy new year!

As we enter 2025 I'm retaining one new year's resolution that I've consistently failed over previous years - 
to go outside every day.
Typically there are only a few days I don't manage it, and those are usually when I'm feeling unwell, so while 
"failed" could be argued I see it as a good lifestyle choice to keep it going.

Sophie and I are adding another resolution that we would like to convert into a habit - to do "one new thing" 
each week.
That could be as simple as trying a new restaurant or as big as running a marathon (unlikely as it is).

I've started building up a list of ideas and have quite a few in mind, although most are things I would already 
like to do and this is just a nudge to get cracking, rather than pushing the limits of my comfort zone.

To get this started I've decided to write my first ever blog post, the one you are reading now!
In order to post this I've already had to set up a blog, helped massively by finding the 
[right article](https://shahrajat.com/2016-06-22-install-jekyll-subdirectory-blog-github-pages/).

There's plenty of work to incorporate the blog into my website's style, and also plenty of posts to write.
Here's to many more.
